# Support Knowledge Base - Sample Document

## Password Reset

If you forget your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login screen. A reset link will be sent to your registered email and expires after 1 hour.

## Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription anytime from Account Settings. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle, and no partial refunds are issued.

## Data Export

Users can export their account data as a CSV file from the Privacy tab in settings. Export requests are processed within 24 hours and a download link is emailed once ready.
